Understanding Teen Mental Health

Mental health is as important as physical health. Teens face unique challenges — academic pressure, social media influence, sleep disruption, and identity development. Our AI models analyze behavioral patterns to provide early intervention signals.

😴

Sleep Hygiene

Teens need 8-10 hours of sleep. Screen exposure before bed disrupts melatonin production. Our data shows sleep deficit is the #1 predictor of depression risk.

📱

Digital Wellbeing

Excessive social media (>3 hrs/day) correlates with 40% higher anxiety levels. Mindful usage and screen-time limits significantly reduce mental health risk.

🏃

Physical Activity

30+ minutes of daily exercise reduces depression risk by 25%. Movement releases endorphins and improves sleep quality — two key protective factors.

🗣️

Social Connection

Low social interaction strongly predicts mental health decline. Encourage face-to-face interactions, group activities, and open conversations with trusted adults.

📚

Academic Balance

Academic pressure is a key stress driver for teens. Healthy study habits, realistic goal-setting, and stress-management techniques are essential for balance.

🆘

When to Seek Help

If a teen shows persistent sadness, withdrawal, appetite changes, or declining performance for 2+ weeks, professional support is recommended. Early intervention saves lives.
